# Decision tree for Alexia — PROPOSAL (not final)

Status: **approved as a base and integrated** into `core/data/trees/alexia.json` (`alexia-v2`, issue #25), including extensions A (node-level `assumption`) and B (`loan_change`), plus `cancel_one_off` so "drop out after year 1" pays no later tuition. Every 🆕 ⚠️ figure is an editable assumption with `verified: false`. The p50 / short. / health figures below were computed with Monte Carlo for the proposal; the app now shows deterministic results first (Monte Carlo only behind the uncertainty lens), computed live by the engine, so read the tables for the trade-offs, not as the numbers on screen.

Constraints followed: 3 levels max, 3 options per node, every option grounded in the UBS scenario catalog (`vendor/ubs-kit/Data/scenario-catalog.csv`, ids `Sxxx`) and in Alexia's situation (20, student in Lausanne, CHF 8,000 savings, CHF 1,200 student job, CHF 1,500 monthly costs; UBS synthetic profile P00001).

Design principles:

- **Siblings are real alternatives to the same question** ("what do I do with my surplus?", "how do I fund the master's?"), so comparing them reveals one trade-off at a time.
- **One option per level is the "plain" reference** (no extra change), so the delta of the other two is easy to read.
- **Each node changes few things** and says them in `changes` (generated by the engine from the interventions).
- Numbers below are **preliminary engine outputs** for this proposal (seed 42, 1,000 paths, default assumptions as of 21 Nov 2026), computed from a scratch copy of the tree; they will move if Juan changes the content or the assumptions.

## Legend

Intervention notation: `income = 4,200 [m0→]` sets net income from month 0 (0-based) onwards; `[m0→m24)` = months 0 to 23; `+=` adds; `×` multiplies; `cost 2,500 @m12` = one-off cost in month 12; `loan 15,000 @2% pay 300 from m24` = new loan.

Metrics: **p50** = median net worth after 10 years (CHF, nominal); **min cash** = lowest cash balance on the reference path (negative = unfunded shortfall); **short.** = share of simulated futures where cash goes below zero at least once; **health** = engine health score 0-100.

Existing assumptions reused (all in `core/data/assumptions_ch.json` or the tree file, with sources): `AS-alexia-work-salary` 4,200, `AS-alexia-work-rent` 1,100, `AS-alexia-student-job` 1,200, `AS-alexia-master-tuition` 2,500/yr (EPFL invoice CHF 1,560/yr verified; UBS 8,000 high case), `AS-alexia-master-salary` 4,800, `AS-alexia-abroad-salary` 3,700, `AS-alexia-move-cost` 6,500 (UBS starter kit), plus macro defaults (inflation, wages, returns, tax, unemployment insurance).

**New assumptions this proposal needs** are marked 🆕 with their proposed source. ⚠️ = not verified yet (would be labelled `verified: false` / modelling choice in the data file).

**Trade-off:** how to bridge the cash gap. That last result depends on 🆕 ⚠️ "a 60% student job pays 60% of the junior salary" (2,520), which should become an editable assumption with a range (e.g. 1,800-2,800): it is exactly the kind of thing the tornado should expose.
🆕 ⚠️ study loan terms (2%, 300/month): modelling choice; cantonal study loans in Vaud are a better source to cite (Office cantonal des bourses d'études, https://www.vd.ch), to verify.

## Weather: the most instructive stress tests per branch

Measured on the proposal tree (1,000 paths). "Δ p50" = change in median 10-year net worth; "Δ short." = change in the share of futures that run out of cash.

| Branch | Weather (suggested parameters) | What it shows | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Work, esp. 1.2 vs 1.1 | **Market crash late** (`market_crash`, `start_month` 108) vs early (60) | Sequence risk: the same crash costs more when it comes just before you need the money | 1.2: Δ p50 −20.0k at m108 vs −11.5k at m60 |
| 1 Work, 1.2 vs 1.1 | **Compound crisis** (`job_loss` + `market_crash`, both at m60; catalog S110) | Under a double shock, investing and cash end up level (112.5k vs 113.8k): diversification of risks, not just returns | |
| 1.3 Car loan | **Job loss early** (`job_loss`, m6) | Fixed debt payments turn a job loss into a cash risk | short. 1% → 5.5% (1.1 stays at 0%) |
| 1.1.3 Work 80% | **Inflation** (`inflation`, m0, 24 months) | Thin margins are the most exposed to prices rising faster than wages | short. +33 pts |
| 2 Master's | **Recession at graduation** (`recession`, `start_month` 24) or **job loss at graduation** (`job_loss`, m24) | The risk of the master's is entering the job market in a bad year | 2.1: Δ p50 −6.6k / −7.0k |
| 2.3 Part-time | **Job loss** (m24) | The "safe" route depends on keeping the part-time job | short. 6% → 18% |
| 3 Abroad, 3.1 vs 3.2 | **Job loss right after moving** (`job_loss`, m3) | The buffer left after paying the move decides everything | short. 3.1: 25% vs 3.2: 2% |
| 3.2.2 Sabbatical | **Job loss** (m12, default) | Taking a break and losing the job compound | short. +33 pts |

Recommendation for the demo: show **one** weather per branch, chosen from the table: "market crash in year 9" on 1.2, "recession at graduation" on 2, "job loss right after moving" on 3.
