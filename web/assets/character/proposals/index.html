Character: 3 proposals (v3, bubbles)

The tree already lives on the page, so the guide is a simple, friendly bubble. Each proposal in 3 health states (healthy · fair · weak) and 2 poses (idle · explaining). Health shows in colour, shape (it deflates) and expression.

Bo

“A light, bouncy bubble: floats next to your tree, explains what each branch means, and never tells you which one to pick.”

Pure circle with a glossy shine. The simplest silhouette: easy to animate (bounce, squash, glide).

Lumi

“A soft little blob with a tiny sprout on top: curious, patient, and happy to show you every path before you choose.”

Gumdrop-shaped blob with a tiny two-leaf sprout that droops when health drops. Links the character to the tree without being a tree.

Dew

“A friendly drop of water: it keeps your tree growing by helping you understand it, one branch at a time.”

Drop shape (soft point on top). Metaphor: water for the tree. Squashes like a puddle when health is weak.

Row 1: idle · Row 2: explaining (leans and points right with a floating hand, mouth open). The final version will be animated (float, blink, squash, glide to the leaf it explains).
