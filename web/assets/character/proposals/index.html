Character: 3 proposals (v2, little humanoid trees)

Each in 3 health states (healthy · fair · weak) and 2 poses (idle · explaining). Bark body, branch arms with twig fingers, root feet and leaves on the head; each with its own face, silhouette, palette and name.

Sprig

“A freshly sprouted little tree, curious and cheerful: shows you what happens on every branch, but the choice is always yours.”

Baby tree: big round head, two-leaf sprout, big dark eyes. Warm bark with soft grain.

Maple

“A calm young tree who speaks slowly: puts your numbers in order and tells you what could happen, without pushing you anywhere.”

Young tree: leafy canopy on top of the head (thins out as health drops), moss on the shoulder, bark brows, golden fruits = savings.

Bea

“A small, bright birch with pale bark: loves asking why and walks you through every path calmly, with the data.”

Birch: white bark with dark horizontal marks, slender body, two little branches on the head. The most distinctive silhouette.

Row 1: idle · Row 2: explaining (arm pointing right holding a leaf, mouth open). The final version will be animated (blink, swaying leaves, walking).
