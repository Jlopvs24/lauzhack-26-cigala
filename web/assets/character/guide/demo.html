Lumi, the guide

A soft glowing orb that floats next to the tree and explains it. It never recommends or decides. Its colour, glow and face follow the health of the selected node. Bring the mouse close (it watches you and slightly dodges), click it (spin and hop), leave it alone for 25 s (it dozes off), or use the controls.
